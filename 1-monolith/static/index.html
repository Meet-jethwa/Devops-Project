Crop stages

One crop, changing needs.

Use these as orientation points for a conversation, not as fixed field prescriptions.

Germination

Indicative stage window

Low to moderate water need; protect emerging growth from stress.

Tillering

Indicative stage window

Moderate water need; steady field moisture supports new shoots.

Grand growth

Indicative stage window

Higher water need; monitor moisture consistently across the field.

Maturity

Indicative stage window

Moderate water need; avoid extremes and observe crop condition.

Ripening

Indicative stage window

Lower water need; use crop and soil observations to guide decisions.

Indicative only; not an agronomic recommendation.
